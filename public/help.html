Help centre

How can we help?

How do I book a PG?

Open any listing, choose a room type with vacancy, pick your move-in date and send a booking request. The owner approves or declines it, and you can follow the status under Dashboard → My bookings.

Is there any brokerage or fee?

No. StayNest is free for students. You pay rent and deposit directly to the PG owner as agreed with them.

Can I cancel a booking request?

Yes — from Dashboard → My bookings, cancel any pending or approved request. If it was approved, the bed becomes available to other students again.

How do complaints work?

Raise a complaint from the Reviews & Support page. The owner is notified, can reply with a note and moves it through Open, In progress and Resolved.

I own a PG. How do I list it?

Create an owner account, then use My listings → Add a PG. You set room types, rent, availability, facilities and house rules, and can hide or republish at any time.

Can anyone write a review?

Any logged-in student can leave one review per PG (and edit it later). Owners cannot review their own listings.
